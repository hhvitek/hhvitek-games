- [ ] Reversi: chosen by the agent (2026-11-12), keep? Classic rules (8 × 8, black starts, a pass when there is no move, the game ends when nobody can move, more discs wins, empty squares are not counted); the starter alternates every game and plays black; the board is never turned. Two levels as in Dáma: "Běžný hráč" ★☆ (default: its move and the best reply judged only by a square-weight table, ±12 noise, in 15 % the second best move) and "Mistr" ★★ (iterative deepening PVS with a transposition table, fewest-replies-first ordering, an evaluation of mobility, corners, X/C squares, edge stability, frontier, parity and late disc count; exact to the end of the game when the budget reaches it, usually from about 14 empty squares). Themes Papír (default), Zelené sukno, Dřevo; "Ukazovat možné tahy" on; the computer's last disc has a red dot; discs turn over one after another (3D flip). - [ ] Lodě: chosen by the agent (2026-11-12), keep? A 10 × 10 sea (A–J, 1–10), ships straight and never touching (not even at a corner); fleet "Pět lodí" 5 + 4 + 3 + 3 + 2 (default) or "Deset lodí" 4 + 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 (the school paper variant); "Po zásahu střílíš znovu" on by default for both sides (off = strictly one shot each); the water around a sunk ship is marked by itself on both seas (around a hit, diagonally, it is not: the player works that out); the starter alternates every game (the first game is the player's). Placing: the fleet starts at random on the big sea, drag a ship to move it (a green or red shadow, the forbidden zone shaded), tap it to turn it round the tapped square (it slides along when it does not fit), Zamíchat, Zpět (only while placing), Hrát; shots cannot be undone. While placing, a new opponent level or "again" applies at once and keeps the arranged ships (only another fleet deals again). In battle the computer's sea is big (cells about 34 px at 393 px width, 27 px at 360 × 640) and yours is at least half that size below it (bigger on tall phones: 20 px cells at 393 × 873), next to the opponent's panel (face, stars, whose turn, its ships as bars, your shot count); a short wide window puts them side by side. Poradit = the Mistr's square (a game with advice keeps no record). Three levels, fair play (only what its own shots told it): Začátečník ★☆☆ (random, 70 % of the time a square next to a hit), Zkušený hráč ★★☆ (default; checkerboard of the smallest ship afloat, then finishes a hit ship along its line), Mistr ★★★ (counts every place each ship afloat could still lie and shoots at the likeliest square). Measured with `tools/lode-test.js --games 1000` (shots to sink a fleet alone): Pět lodí 63.5 / 43.2 / 38.6, Deset lodí 63.7 / 58.4 / 55.1; matches with "again", 100 games: Zkušený beats Začátečník 93 : 7, Mistr beats Zkušený 82 : 18 (Deset lodí only 68 : 32 and 58 : 42: the four one-square ships make it a game of luck; a Mistr sampling whole fleets could do better). Themes Papír (default), Moře, Sešit; "Rychlost soupeře" Pomalu (default) / Rychle. Statistics per level and fleet: played, won, the fewest shots of a win without Poradit.
