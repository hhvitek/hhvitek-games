- [ ] Věžičky (hexa sort): chosen by the agent (2026-11-12), keep? The name "Věžičky" (short for the top bar). A honeycomb of 24 flat hexagons (columns of 4, 5, 6, 5, 4), drawn from the front and slightly above so the stacks are little towers; three stacks of 2–6 tiles in 1–3 colours in the tray, a new three when all are placed. The gathering rule: after a stack is placed, neighbouring stacks with the same top colour gather that colour on one of them, until no two neighbours share a top colour: the target is a stack of that colour only, else the one with the longest top run, else the one just placed (a neighbour that does not touch the target sends its tiles over the placed one); the stack touched last goes on gathering with its own neighbours, and the colour that comes up under moved tiles gathers too. 10 or more of one colour on top vanish (merges first, then all full towers at once): every tile is a point, the second vanishing in the same move counts twice, the third three times. Difficulty = the colours at the start: Lehká 4, Střední 5 (default), Těžká 6; every 150 points one more colour comes (when a new three is dealt), at most 8 (červená, modrá, žlutá, zelená, fialová, tyrkysová, oranžová, růžová); without the growing colours a careful player never lost (simulated). Simulated with `tools/vezicky-test.js` (moves / points): Střední random moves about 60 / 140, Poradit about 170 / 600; Lehká 100 / 300 and 210 / 780; Těžká 43 / 70 and 155 / 530. The game ends when the board is full; no time pressure. Record per difficulty; Zpět up to 100 moves back, also after the end (the same new stacks come again, from the seed); Poradit (the best single move by points, empty cells and hidden colours) frames a stack and a cell, a tap on the cell plays it, and it does not affect the record.
